A badge on your Agentypical listing that says an AI agent really did sign up for your service and finish a core task, and that we checked the result ourselves.

How it works

  1. You ask for a check. Humanless runs real agents through your sign-up and one core task, and verifies the end state independently (API, database or test inbox).

  3. We re-check every month. If a re-check fails, we tell you and the badge comes off until it passes again.
